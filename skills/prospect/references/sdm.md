## Coverage area

In person (about an hour's drive): Sandusky, Huron, Castalia, Milan, Berlin Heights, Vermilion, Wakeman, Norwalk, Monroeville, Bellevue, Clyde, Willard, New London, Fremont, Tiffin, Port Clinton, Marblehead, Catawba Island, Oak Harbor, Kelleys Island, Put-in-Bay, Amherst, Oberlin, Lorain, Elyria, Toledo, Cleveland.

Remote: Columbus, Akron, Canton, Lima, and anywhere else in the U.S. Prefer in-person towns. Being local is SDM's strongest selling point, and it's weaker with a business two hours away.

Seasonal note: Put-in-Bay, Kelleys Island, Marblehead, Catawba and the Cedar Point area are tourist economies. In late fall and winter many owners are planning for the next season, which is a good time to pitch a redesign or booking system, but some businesses don't check messages until spring.
