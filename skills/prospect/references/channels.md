# Sending on each channel

All sending goes through Justin's signed-in browser (Claude in Chrome or the equivalent). Work in a new tab, not one he has open. Never sign in, sign out, or change any account setting. If a site asks for a login, stop and ask Justin to sign in.

## Email (Gmail)

1. Send from stonedragonmedia@gmail.com, always. Chrome is often signed in to several Google accounts, and Gmail opens whichever is the default. Open `https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=stonedragonmedia@gmail.com` to load that account directly, then check the account avatar or email shown in the top right. If that account isn't signed in, stop and ask Justin to sign in. Don't fall back to another account.
2. Compose. Check the From line once more (if the account has send-as aliases, pick stonedragonmedia@gmail.com). Fill To, Subject, and body exactly as approved. Paste plain text, without formatting.
3. If Gmail inserted a signature, compare it with the approved sign-off. Delete it if it contains links, a web address, or social icons, or if it duplicates the sign-off.
4. If it's after 6 p.m., before 7 a.m., or a weekend, offer to use Gmail's schedule send for the next weekday at about 9 a.m. A message read on a Tuesday morning gets a better reading than one buried under the weekend.
5. Send, wait for "Message sent," and record it.

## Website contact form

1. Open the form on the business's own site. If the form is on a third-party service (Wix, Squarespace, JotForm, a scheduling tool), that's fine as long as the business's site is what links to it.
2. Fill only the fields approved in step 6 of SKILL.md. Use Justin's name and phone from `sender.md`, and stonedragonmedia@gmail.com as the email so replies land in SDM's inbox. If a field is required but wasn't in the approval (a dropdown such as "reason for contact"), ask Justin which value to use before submitting. A submitted form can't be taken back.
3. Don't tick newsletter or marketing opt-in boxes.
4. If there's a CAPTCHA or "I'm not a robot" check, stop. Tell Justin the form is filled in and ask him to complete the check and click Submit himself.
5. After submitting, confirm the thank-you message or page. If the page shows an error, or the form clears with no confirmation, report that and don't record the message as sent.

## Facebook

1. Use the identity in `sender.md`. Facebook usually doesn't let a business Page start a conversation, so the default is Justin's personal profile messaging the business's Page. If `sender.md` says to send as the Stone Dragon Media Page and Facebook won't allow it, tell Justin instead of switching identities on your own.
2. Open the business's Page and click Message. Confirm the name in the chat header is the business you researched.
3. Paste the approved text and send. Facebook sometimes turns a phone number into a link. That's fine.
4. Messages from someone the Page hasn't talked to may land in its "Message requests." Say so in the report so Justin isn't surprised by a slow reply.

## Instagram and LinkedIn

Only when the business lists one of them for inquiries and none of the higher channels exists. Use the same message as Facebook, trimmed if needed. Send as Justin, check the recipient's name before sending, and do nothing else on the profile.
