# Verification traps

These mistakes have each turned up in real Firelands Current sourcing. Check every lead against this list before it goes into the packet. `docs/news-sources.md` in the repo has notes on particular sources; this file covers the general patterns.

## Dates

- **Confirm the year on the page itself.** Search summaries, page titles, URLs and homepage promos keep last year's dates. A Squarespace URL can carry an old year while the event header shows this year's. A festival page can still be titled with last spring's event name.
- **The posting date isn't the announcement date.** A web post on Oct. 12 can carry an image of a Sept. 30 release. Report the date the news happened, not the date you found it.
- **Check the weekday against the date.** Organizer pages get it wrong ("Thursday, Oct. 7" when Oct. 7 is a Wednesday). Use the date, and note the conflict.
- **Drop anything already over** unless it explains something current. "This month" in a search result can mean a year ago.
- Calendar entries can show midnight or all-day placeholders. That doesn't mean an event starts at midnight or runs all day.

## Times, money and access

- **A gate or doors time isn't a performance time, and a calendar window isn't a duration.** A 5:30–8 p.m. listing doesn't mean the parade lasts that long.
- **Don't infer an end time.** Leave it blank.
- **Free admission doesn't make everything free.** Tours, wristbands, parking and activities are charged separately. A missing price isn't evidence of free entry.
- **"Starting at" isn't the checkout total.** Say "starting prices" and note that fees aren't stated.
- **Check availability where tickets are actually sold.** A homepage can say "tickets on sale" while the ticket page says sold out.
- Age, eligibility, accessibility and registration terms often differ between two pages from the same organizer. Report the conflict or leave the term out. Never pick one.

## Claims

- **Projected jobs aren't filled jobs, and announced investment isn't completed investment.** Attribute projections to whoever made them.
- **A scheduled completion isn't a confirmed reopening, and a hoped-for date isn't a promise.**
- **An award, prize or recognition doesn't establish the underlying facts again.** If the honored project was announced earlier, the news is the award.
- **A public notice establishes a hearing, not its outcome.** Attribute findings (an "unsafe structure") to the body that made them, and give the person affected a chance to respond.
- **When two official pages disagree** (a fee on a dated notice versus an older paragraph), report both or tell readers to confirm. Don't silently choose one.

## Source quality

- **Primary first.** Agencies, organizers and documents are sources. The Sandusky Register, Norwalk Reflector, radio station recaps and Google News results are leads: find the underlying record, don't reuse their reporting, and never get around a paywall.
- **Official social accounts count as sources**, but link the agency's website or document when one exists. Open the post itself and expand the full text. Facebook search ranks pages by distance and name, so try other wordings before deciding a Page doesn't exist.
- **Residents' comments, Facebook groups and Reddit are leads only.** Never quote private individuals from them.
- **Read the image.** Health departments and chambers often put the details in a flyer or a picture of the press release. Open the image, and put any text it carries into the story or caption.
- **Watch for look-alike places.** Huron, South Dakota's library; Sandusky County versus the city of Sandusky; Huron the city versus Huron County. The newsroom skill's house style lists the collisions.
- PDFs (auditor releases, ODNR charts, brochures) need a careful read: `pdftotext -layout` keeps columns aligned. Read the year printed inside each brochure.

## Keep the evidence

Save a copy of every page, PDF or image you rely on into `evidence/` (HTML, text or a screenshot). Notices disappear and pages change, and the editor or a correction may need to see what the source said on the day.
