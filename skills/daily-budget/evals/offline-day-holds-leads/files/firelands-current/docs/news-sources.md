# News sources

Coverage area: Erie, Huron, Ottawa, and Sandusky counties. Facts come from the primary source; a resident's post is a lead, never a source.

## Reliable and open

- **Port Clinton City Council** (https://www.portclinton.example/council): agendas Thursday before each meeting, minutes about two weeks after. Trap: the agenda page keeps last year's PDFs at the top; check the year in the filename.
- **Ottawa County commissioners** (https://www.co.ottawa.example/commissioners): weekly resolutions. Trap: resolution numbers restart every January.
- **Sandusky Library events** (https://www.sanduskylib.example/events): reliable times and registration links.
