Sourdough questions

Why is my sourdough so dense?

Usually the dough is underproofed. Let the bulk ferment run until the dough has grown by about half.

Can I use all-purpose flour?

Yes. Bread flour gives a taller loaf, but all-purpose works with slightly lower hydration.

How often do I feed my starter?

Once a day at room temperature, or once a week if you keep it in the fridge.
