Field notebooks made for birders

Every Larkspur notebook is sewn by hand in our Asheville bindery from waterproof stone paper, so a sudden downpour at the marsh won't smear a morning's count. The pages are ruled for species, counts, weather, and a quick sketch, with a pocket in the back for loose checklists.

Three sizes

The Pocket fits a shirt pocket, the Field fits a vest, and the Ledger stays in the car for the season's totals. All three ship free anywhere in the United States.
