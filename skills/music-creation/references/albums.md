# Optional EP and album planning

Use this mode for collection-level requests or relevant cross-track context, not as a prerequisite for a song.

Return the requested concept and a compact track map (working title, angle, vocal or instrumental, energy, groove, relation to adjacent tracks), with undecided titles and lengths marked provisional. Use callbacks only when they acquire meaning; don't insert a reference to fill a motif table.

For single-track revision, read only the sibling material needed for context and edit only the authorized scope. Preserve deliberate changes of voice, language, tone, and genre. For real people and events, distinguish supplied facts from invention, and verify claims when accuracy matters.

Artwork, mixing and mastering, promotion, distribution, uploads, and release management are out of scope.
