# Firelands Current house style

These rules override AP. Where this file is silent, follow `ap-style.md`. Don't guess at local facts that aren't here; mark them `[NEED: ...]`.

## Decisions

- **Publication name:** `Firelands Current` on first reference, `the Current` after.
- **Oxford comma:** no, per AP. Use one only when a series would be unclear without it.
- **Percent:** `%` with figures (`5%`). Use "percentage points" for differences.
- **More than / over:** write "more than" with numbers (`more than 100 people`), never "over".
- **Courtesy titles:** none, in any story, including obituaries.
- **Datelines:** none for stories in the coverage area. Use an AP dateline for stories reported from outside it.
- **Time references:** use the day of the week within seven days, and the date beyond that (AP).
- **Corrections:** fix the error and put a dated correction note at the top of the story that says what was wrong and what's right. Never make a silent change to a fact.

## Sourcing and naming

- **Anonymous sources:** only with editor approval, and with a stated reason the source can't be named. Flag `LEGAL_RISK` or `UNVERIFIED_CLAIM` so an editor sees it.
- **Suspects:** name them once formal charges are filed. People arrested but not charged stay unnamed.
- **Juveniles:** not named unless charged or tried as adults.
- **Sexual assault victims:** never named without their consent.
- **Mug shots:** rarely, and only with editor approval, for serious violent crimes or when public safety is at stake (for example, a suspect at large). Otherwise, don't request or suggest one.

## Coverage area

The core area is Huron and Erie counties plus the historic Firelands townships in Ottawa County (Danbury, part of Catawba Island) and Ashland County (Ruggles). Neighboring counties (Seneca, Sandusky, Lorain, Richland, the rest of Ottawa) are covered as stories warrant. Name the county on first reference to any township or small community.

## Local glossary

### Names that collide

- **Firelands:** capitalize. `the Firelands` is the region. Don't confuse it with Firelands Local School District, Firelands Regional Medical Center, Firelands Health, or BGSU Firelands.
- **Sandusky:** the city is the Erie County seat. Sandusky County is a different county, with Fremont as its seat. Write out `Sandusky Bay` and `Sandusky River` in full.
- **Huron:** the city is in Erie County. Huron County has Norwalk as its seat. There's also a Huron Township (Erie County) and the Huron River. Always make clear which one you mean.
- **Bellevue** spans Huron, Sandusky, Seneca, and Erie counties. **Milan** (village) spans Erie and Huron counties. **Vermilion** spans Erie and Lorain counties.
- Several township names match a village or city: Greenwich, New London, Norwalk, Wakeman, New Haven, Huron, Milan, Vermilion. Write `Norwalk Township` in full every time.

### Government

- **Counties:** `Huron County commissioners` and `Erie County commissioners` (lowercase "commissioners"). Capitalize `Board of Commissioners` only in the full formal name. Elected county officials take a capitalized title before a name: `Huron County Auditor Jane Smith`, `Erie County Sheriff John Doe`. Lowercase the title alone: `the auditor`.
- **Sandusky** has a City Commission and a city manager. The commission president serves as ex officio mayor. Write `Sandusky City Commission` and `City Commission President Jane Smith`. Don't call it a city council.
- **Huron** (city) has a council-manager government: a city council, a mayor, and a city manager.
- **Norwalk, Willard, and Bellevue:** city council and mayor.
- **Villages:** village council and mayor (`Milan Village Council`).
- **Townships:** a board of three trustees plus a fiscal officer. Write `Norwalk Township trustees` and `trustee Jane Smith` (lowercase, since it isn't a formal title before a name).
- **Courts:** `Huron County Common Pleas Court`, `Erie County Common Pleas Court`, `Norwalk Municipal Court`, `Sandusky Municipal Court`. Use `Common Pleas Judge Jane Smith` and the plain `the court` on second reference.
